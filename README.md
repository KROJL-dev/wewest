
# WeWest Mobile App 🇺🇦

This project is a comprehensive platform designed to enhance your dining and social experiences. Users can effortlessly explore and choose restaurants based on their preferences, stay updated on the latest events, and discover exciting activities happening nearby.

## Features 📱

- Restaurant Discovery: Browse and filter restaurants by cuisine, location, ratings, and more.
- Event Updates: Stay informed about upcoming events, special offers, and exclusive dining experiences.
- Personalized Recommendations: Get tailored suggestions based on your tastes and interests.
- User-Friendly Interface: Navigate seamlessly with an intuitive and visually appealing design.
